# Project Depot 0.4.0

Release date: 2026-11-24. Tag `v0.4.0`.

The minor number goes up because this release adds features and removes some (see
[versioning](https://github.com/OmsiModsBR/depot/blob/v0.4.0/docs/VERSIONING.md)). The historical
baseline is still openOMSI `v0.2.16`. Full list of pull requests:
[`v0.3.0...v0.4.0`](https://github.com/OmsiModsBR/depot/compare/v0.3.0...v0.4.0); details in
[CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/OmsiModsBR/depot/blob/v0.4.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Download

- `depot-0.4.0-windows-x64.zip`: game and launcher (Windows x64 only). Project Depot needs an
  original OMSI 2 installation and includes no game content.

## What's new

- **Two vehicle physics engines** (Settings → Simulation → Vehicle physics): **OMSI Classic**, the
  default, keeps OMSI 2's rules so mods behave as their authors tuned them; **Project Depot
  Physics** adds Depot's own improvements. A bus with a `[depot_physics]` section always runs the
  Depot engine and is marked **DEPOT PHYSICS** in the launcher.
- **Project Depot Physics:** where the passengers sit and stand moves the centre of gravity and the
  inertia of the bus; tyres hold the road axle by axle; any axle can be made steerable with the new
  `achse_steer_angle` and `achse_steer_centring` parameters (tag axles, twin steer).
- **Grip per tyre** from the ground under each wheel and the weather, in both engines; scripts can
  read the ground kind with `Axle_SurfaceID`.
- **Trolleybuses without plugins:** poles raised and lowered by hand onto the map's wires, shoes that
  follow the wire, switches and crossings, wire sounds and sparks, and poles that leave the wire.
  Existing trolleybus scripts run unchanged.
- **Graphics modes simplified:** Vanilla is now drawn by the Direct3D 9Ex renderer on Windows;
  Vanilla+ and Enhanced stay. Ambient occlusion and clouds switch on and off at once from the in-game
  menu, whose graphics list now matches the launcher's.
- **Sky:** the real stars and constellations from the game's `stars.dat`, the sun and the moon (with
  its phase), and the sun's glare, in every graphics mode.
- **Vanilla (Direct3D 9Ex):** wet roads whose puddles mirror the street, raindrops on glass with the
  wiper's sweep, the weather's clouds, render scale, a Performance section and effort-saving options
  for slower computers.
- **Information bar:** pedals, road grade and a skid lamp in every layout, and one family of line
  icons for the bar and the maps.
- **Driving and traffic:** Add vehicle can place a bus at a map entry point or at the mouse cursor; a
  *Make everyone get off* button; the cursor stays in the window while the mouse steers; AI emergency
  vehicles pass red lights and other cars make way for them.
- **Saved situations** keep the passengers, an articulated bus's bend, the brake pedal and the
  steering.
- **Logs:** four log levels and per-part levels (`--log-level`, `--log-channels`), 30 MB per file,
  your user folder written as `USER_FOLDER`, one `launcher.log`; new LOGS and PRIVACY pages.
- **Removed:** the Enhanced+ graphics mode (a saved `enhanced_plus` opens in Enhanced), the separate
  wgpu Vanilla look, the DirectX9 Snow option (snow now follows the weather) and the
  `OMSI_ENHANCED`/`OMSI_NO_ENHANCED` variables (use `OMSI_GRAPHICS`).

## Fixes

- **AI traffic** gets denser with the traffic options again, lane by lane, and no longer locks up
  where a map lays two paths over each other.
- **Passengers** get off AI timetable buses again; the cash desk's coins stay in the bus the driver
  leaves.
- **Articulated buses:** the rear section of a pusher bus no longer drifts in pitch; with OMSI
  Classic the section behind a joint is put back on it every frame; a bus no longer stands nose or
  tail down because of where its `[cog]` lies.
- **Driver** no longer sits hunched over the wheel and sits back against the seat.
- **Scripts:** a text written over several lines inside one pair of quotes is read whole.
- **Enhanced:** true lamp colours, round far coronas instead of squares, envmaps on bump-mapped
  materials, lit text at night, paint with a weak envmap reflects again, and the same reflection
  under a vehicle's roof.
- **Bus text** (`[useTextTexture]`) takes the ambient occlusion of its sign and no longer writes
  depth.
- **Vanilla (Direct3D 9Ex):** blank screenshots, misplaced terrain paint on NVIDIA cards, distant
  textures averaged in linear light, and the blending of light glows.
- **DirectX 12:** no more stack overflow while compiling the sky and cloud shaders at start-up.
- **Screenshots** hold the scene alone, without the interface.
- **Performance:** the minimap no longer stutters every 600 m or so; the first AI cars are placed
  during the loading screen; the launcher reads its vehicle and map lists once.
- **Launcher:** the Vehicles and Maps pages take every new list, very large maps draw their roads,
  the automatic depot file picks the right `.hof`, Setup shows the real game version, and long
  vehicle names wrap.
- **Trolleybuses:** a switch on the wire beside the bus's own no longer slows it or drops its poles.

## Known limitations

- Most physics and rendering changes are checked by automated tests, traces and reference pictures,
  not by a recorded side-by-side comparison with OMSI 2 in the game. Treat them as not demonstrated
  1:1.
- Trolleybuses were checked in the game with one articulated trolleybus on one map, not against
  OMSI 2 and its plugin.
- Vanilla (Direct3D 9Ex) is Windows only and runs without VR and triple screen.
- Windows x64 package only; the original OMSI 2 mode in the launcher is still hidden.

## Build and validation

Built with `OPENOMSI_VERSION=0.4.0` from the merge commit of the release pull request; commit,
toolchain, `depot --version` and the SHA256 of the zip are added here when the package is built.
